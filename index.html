PROFILE

About me

I am a PhD candidate working at the intersection of Earth observation big data, artificial intelligence and agricultural sustainability. My research has progressed from digital soil mapping and multi-source spectral fusion to large-area crop monitoring, multimodal satellite time series and generalizable agricultural mapping. I am particularly interested in building scalable Earth-intelligence methods that remain reliable across regions and years and can support agricultural statistics, resource management and food-security decision making.

My current geographic focus spans Xinjiang and Central Asia, where heterogeneous agroclimatic conditions and limited labels provide a natural testbed for label-efficient, multimodal and transferable agricultural AI.

RESEARCH

Research vision

Earth Observation Big Data & GeoAI

Scalable processing and representation learning for multi-source, multi-temporal Earth observation data, with emphasis on Sentinel-1/2, Landsat, hyperspectral observations and cloud-scale geospatial computing.

Generalizable Crop Intelligence

Label-efficient crop mapping through multimodal phenology, weak supervision, few-shot adaptation, domain adaptation/generalization and emerging geospatial foundation-model representations.

Digital Soil & Agroecosystem Monitoring

Multi-source spectral reconstruction, soil–vegetation signal separation and machine learning for soil organic matter, nitrogen, moisture and related crop–soil indicators.

Food Security & Sustainable Agrifood Systems

Connecting Earth observation and AI products with agricultural monitoring, climate resilience, SDG 2 assessment and evidence-based decision support across data-scarce agricultural regions.

Current doctoral direction. I am building a cross-regional crop-mapping framework using 2025 field samples from Kashgar (southern Xinjiang), 2026 field samples from northern Xinjiang, historical crop maps and multi-year Sentinel-1/Sentinel-2 time series. The long-term goal is to move beyond single-region classification toward robust agricultural Earth intelligence that can transfer from Xinjiang to Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and other data-scarce regions.
