PROFILE

About me

I am a PhD candidate working at the intersection of Earth observation big data, artificial intelligence and agricultural sustainability. My work spans digital soil mapping, multi-source spectral fusion, large-area crop monitoring, multimodal satellite time series and generalizable agricultural mapping.

My current geographic focus spans Xinjiang and Central Asia, where contrasting agroclimatic zones and limited labels provide a natural testbed for label-efficient, multimodal and transferable agricultural AI.

RESEARCH

Research vision

Earth Observation Big Data & GeoAI

Scalable processing and representation learning for multi-source, multi-temporal EO data, emphasizing Sentinel-1/2, Landsat, hyperspectral observations and cloud-scale geospatial computing.

Generalizable Crop Intelligence

Label-efficient crop mapping through multimodal phenology, weak supervision, few-shot adaptation, domain adaptation/generalization and geospatial foundation-model representations.

Digital Soil & Agroecosystem Monitoring

Spectral reconstruction, soil-vegetation signal separation and machine learning for soil organic matter, nitrogen, moisture and crop-soil indicators.

Food Security & Sustainable Agrifood Systems

Connecting EO and AI products with agricultural monitoring, climate resilience, SDG 2 assessment and evidence-based decision support.

Current doctoral direction. Cross-regional crop mapping using 2025 Kashgar field samples, 2026 northern Xinjiang samples, historical crop maps, Sentinel-1/Sentinel-2 time series and climate/soil covariates, with eventual transfer toward Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and other data-scarce agricultural regions.
